Backtesting Engine

Three modes. Falsification built-in. No look-ahead bias.

Event-Driven

Tick-by-tick simulation. Realistic fills, slippage, latency, partial fills. Slowest but most accurate.

Vectorized

NumPy/Pandas operations. 100-1000x faster. Good for parameter sweeps. Assumes next-bar open fills.

Hybrid

Vectorized for signal gen, event-driven for execution. Best of both. Default for research.

Falsification Battery

PSR, Deflated Sharpe, PBO, Purged CV, Walk-Forward, Monte Carlo. Every backtest runs the full suite.

Attribution

# Brinson-Hood-Beebower
allocation = weight * (benchmark_ret - port_ret)
selection = weight * (port_ret - benchmark_ret)
interaction = allocation * selection
